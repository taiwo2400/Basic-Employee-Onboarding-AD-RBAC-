Incident Resolution Report — NMG-0047
Affected User: Jane Cooper (jcooper@NMG.com)  
Department: Human Resources (Payroll)  
Resolved By: Taiwo Olaofe  
Date: October 16, 2026


Issue Reported
Jane Cooper was unable to access HR shared resources and experienced desktop policy configurations and mapped drives that differed from those of her HR colleagues.


Investigation & Findings
Using Active Directory Users and Computers (ADUC), I identified two account configuration issues:
1. Incorrect OU Placement: Jane's account was located in the Operations Organizational Unit instead of HR.
2. Incorrect Security Group Membership: Jane was assigned to Operations-Users rather than HR-Users.
These configuration errors were consistent with the reported access restrictions and desktop policy discrepancies.


Root Cause
Jane's account had been provisioned under the incorrect department, resulting in improper OU placement and security group membership. This configuration prevented her account from aligning with the intended HR access model.


Corrective Actions
1. Moved Jane's user account from the Operations OU to the HR OU.
2. Removed Jane from the Operations-Users security group.
3. Added Jane to the HR-Users security group.


Verification
Confirmed Jane's updated OU placement in ADUC and verified her membership in HR-Users through the group's Members tab.


Resolution Status
Active Directory account configuration corrected. HR resource access, mapped drives, and applied Group Policy settings require separate end-user verification to confirm complete incident resolution.
